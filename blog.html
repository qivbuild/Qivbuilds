From Planning to Handover

Every project starts with planning We review the plot the applicable bylaws and your requirements then decide the layout floor areas and the exterior and interior design

Next the drawing set is prepared: architectural structural electrical and plumbing drawings with the dimensions and reinforcement details needed for approval and construction

Construction begins with the gray structure: foundation columns beams slabs and masonry built to the drawings with material testing and site supervision

Services and finishing follow: electrical and plumbing installation plaster flooring tiles paint joinery and fixtures completed to the interior and exterior design

Existing buildings are renovated after a condition survey Defects are repaired services are upgraded and the layout and finishes are improved

Qivbuild handles building planning design and construction as one team

Project Management Project Management

Project management covers scope drawings material approvals procurement inspections and payments We prepare a construction schedule from the approved drawings and assign a supervisor to each stage Before every concrete pour reinforcement and formwork are checked against the drawings Progress and variations are reported to the owner in writing

STEP 3

Team Management Team Management

Our architects structural engineers site engineers supervisors and trade crews work from the same approved drawing set Each role has a defined scope: the architect controls the plan the structural engineer controls member sizes and reinforcement and the site engineer controls execution Instructions are always given from the latest drawing revision which prevents errors caused by outdated drawings

Time Management Time Management

Time is planned from the day the drawings are approved Work is sequenced as excavation foundation columns slabs masonry services plaster and finishes with curing and drying time allowed between stages Materials are ordered ahead of each stage so that work is not delayed and the schedule is reviewed weekly against site progress
