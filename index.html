Drawing Plan — Building Planning

Qivbuild — Building Plan Design to Construction

Planning structural design and approval drawings by one team

A building plan is the technical basis of the whole project It fixes the room layout structural system services routes quantities and approval documents before construction starts At Qivbuild the drawings are prepared by the same team that builds so every dimension is checked for constructability and cost

Planning starts with the plot: size shape orientation road width soil condition and the applicable bylaws such as plot coverage setbacks maximum height and parking We work within these limits to give you the best usable area natural light and ventilation for your budget

What the Drawing Set Includes

  • Architectural plans — Floor plans elevations sections and room schedules with dimensions door and window sizes and finish levels
  • Structural drawings — Foundation layout column schedule beam and slab reinforcement details and staircase and tank details designed according to the Building Code of Pakistan and recognized structural codes
  • Electrical plan — Load schedule distribution board location circuits switch socket light fan and AC points and earthing
  • Plumbing plan — Water supply lines drainage sewer connection manhole and septic tank locations water tank and pump positions and gas line routing
  • Approval drawings — Submission drawings in the format required by DHA LDA or TMA with the required notes areas and plot details
  • 3D views — Exterior and interior views used to confirm design decisions before construction

Our Process

1 Site visit and survey — We measure the plot check levels orientation and access and review soil information Your requirements and budget are recorded

2 Concept design — Two or three layout options are prepared with areas and circulation so you can select one

3 Detailed drawings — After the concept is approved we prepare the architectural structural electrical and plumbing drawings and a bill of quantities

4 Approval and construction — Drawings are submitted to the authority After approval the same team builds exactly to the approved drawings

Why Qivbuild for Planning

Our drawings are prepared with site experience so they work in practice: practical column positions correct stair and room dimensions workable service routes and accurate quantities This reduces changes material waste and cost overruns during construction

We plan 5 Marla 10 Marla 1 Kanal and commercial plots in DHA Phase 8 and across Lahore One team handles design and construction so there is no gap between the drawing and the work on site

Gray Structure — Structural Construction

Qivbuild — Structural Construction to Drawings

Gray structure = foundation frame slabs and masonry before finishing

Gray structure is the complete structural work of the building up to the finishing stage: excavation foundation columns beams slabs staircases and brick masonry with plaster and services rough-in Its quality decides the safety and service life of the building so it is built strictly to the structural drawings

Errors at this stage are costly to correct later We therefore check reinforcement formwork levels and alignment before every concrete pour and record the checks

Scope of Gray Structure

  • Excavation and foundation — Excavation to the designed depth compaction plain concrete (PCC) and reinforced footings or raft as per the foundation design
  • Columns — Reinforcement as per the column schedule correct lap lengths and stirrup spacing with plumb checked before pouring
  • Beams and slabs — Formwork bar bending to the drawings concrete cover and placement of electrical conduits and plumbing sleeves before casting
  • Brick masonry — Wall thickness and bonding as per drawings vertical alignment and openings at the designed sizes and levels
  • Staircase and water tanks — Reinforced concrete staircases and underground and overhead tanks with waterproofing
  • Damp-proofing and waterproofing — Damp-proof course at plinth level and waterproofing of roof bathrooms and tanks

Gray Structure Process

1 Excavation and foundation — Setting out from the drawings excavation to the designed depth soil check PCC and foundation concrete

2 Columns and beams — Reinforcement fixing shuttering and concrete with level and plumb checks at each stage

3 Slab and masonry — Slab casting with proper compaction and brickwork under daily supervision

4 Quality control — Concrete cube tests slump tests reinforcement checks and a minimum of 7 days of curing

Why Qivbuild for Gray Structure

We use cement and steel from approved manufacturers follow the specified mix and reinforcement quantities and do not substitute materials without the engineer approval Material receipts and test results are available to the owner

Because the same team prepared the drawings questions on site are resolved quickly and correctly and the building is constructed exactly as designed

Qivbuild — Functional Interior Planning and Finishing

Interior design coordinated with the building services

Interior design covers the planning and finishing of all internal spaces: room layouts furniture positions kitchen and bathroom design joinery ceilings flooring lighting and colours We design the interior together with the building services so that electrical points plumbing and AC are placed correctly before walls are finished

Interior work is planned from measured dimensions so cabinets doors and fixtures fit without adjustment on site

Scope of Interior Work

  • Bedrooms — Wardrobe and bed layout ceiling and lighting design and window placement for natural light and privacy
  • Bathrooms — Tile layout waterproofing slope to drains sanitary fixture positions and ventilation
  • Lighting — General task and accent lighting plans with switching layout and load calculation
  • Finishes — Flooring wall finishes false ceilings paint doors and joinery with material specifications

Interior Design Process

1 Requirement brief — Family size usage and budget are recorded and rooms are measured on site

2 3D layout — Layouts and 3D views are prepared and revised before work begins

3 Materials and execution — Tiles marble wood and paint are specified with quantities and work proceeds room by room under supervision

Why Qivbuild for Interior Design

Interior work is carried out by the same team that built the structure so concealed pipes conduits and structural elements are known and accounted for in the design Finishes are chosen to match your budget and the way each room will be used

Renovation — Repair and Remodelling

Qivbuild — Assessment Repair and Remodelling of Existing Buildings

Condition survey first then repair upgrade and remodelling

Renovation improves an existing building by repairing defects upgrading services and changing the layout or finishes We start with a condition survey so the scope and cost are based on what the building actually needs Cracks dampness leakage and outdated wiring and plumbing are identified before any work is priced

Structural alterations such as removing walls or adding a floor are checked by a structural engineer before work begins so the building remains safe

Scope of Renovation

  • Condition survey — Inspection of structure walls roof plumbing and electrical systems with a written report and photographs
  • Repair — Crack repair damp and seepage treatment roof waterproofing and replacement of damaged wiring and pipes
  • Remodelling — Changing room sizes relocating kitchens and bathrooms and opening or closing walls with structural checks
  • Finishes upgrade — New flooring tiles paint ceilings doors and lighting
  • Strengthening and additions — Column and beam strengthening and new floors or extensions designed and checked against the existing foundation
  • Full renovation — Complete work from structure to finishing when the building needs a comprehensive upgrade

Renovation Process

1 Survey and estimate — The building is inspected defects are listed and a cost estimate is given before work begins

2 Renovation plan — Drawings show what is removed retained and added with a schedule and sequence of work

3 Execution — Work proceeds in sections with dust and debris control and regular progress updates

Why Qivbuild for Renovation

We carry out both design and construction so the existing structure services and new work are coordinated Scope cost and schedule are agreed in writing before work starts

Ask Qivbuild — Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer You can also ask your own question below

How long does a drawing plan take?

Architectural drawings usually take 7 to 15 days depending on plot size and number of floors The layout plan is prepared first followed by the structural electrical and plumbing drawings

What information is needed to start building planning?

Plot size and location the plot map or documents the number of floors and rooms required your budget and any special requirements A site visit is then arranged to measure the plot and check levels access and orientation

What is included in gray structure?

Excavation foundation columns beams slabs staircase brick masonry and plaster with electrical conduits and plumbing sleeves placed during construction Finishing work such as flooring tiles paint and fixtures is not included

How is the cost of interior design calculated?

Cost depends on room sizes materials and the design specification Send your plot size and room list and Qivbuild will give an estimate before the final plan is prepared

What does town planning include?

A master plan of the layout: road network plot divisions parks and open space commercial areas and water supply sewerage and storm water drainage It is prepared for authority approval and phased development

How long does renovation take?

A small renovation takes about 2 to 4 weeks and a full house about 2 to 4 months depending on the scope A condition survey is done first followed by the renovation plan and schedule

Where does Qivbuild work?

Qivbuild works from DHA Phase 8 Lahore across Lahore and nearby cities from drawing plan to construction

Does Qivbuild prepare approval drawings?

Yes We prepare approval and submission drawings for LDA and TMA including the drawings required for NOC

What is the difference between residential and commercial buildings?

Residential buildings are houses and villas planned around room layout privacy and natural light Commercial buildings such as plazas offices and shops are planned for business use with frontage parking fire exits and flexible floor layouts

What does banquet hall planning include?

Hall size and seating capacity stage position guest and service entrances kitchen access parking exits ventilation and lighting

What does market planning include?

Shop sizes and frontage walkway widths loading access parking drainage fire exits and the electrical load of each shop
